Who we built this for

Most people we work with arrive somewhere similar.

Tired in a way sleep doesn't fix. A body that doesn't feel like theirs. Told by more than one doctor that everything looks normal while everything feels off.

Hashimoto's, perimenopause, postpartum, autoimmune, low testosterone, gut chaos nobody could name. The gap between what your labs read and what your body knows is real, and it's what we built this practice to close.

If that sounds like you, you're in the right place.

The Standard

You stop being the patient. You become the person leading their own biology.

Most people arrive hoping someone will finally tell them what's wrong. The people we work with arrive ready to own their body, their data, and their standard. That's where the real change starts.

The Sequence

Five phases. In the order biology demands.

Each phase has a job, a way it feels, and clear signals you're ready to move. Tap through it.

Energy. The floor.

Mitochondria

The energy floor under every cell. If your cells can't make power, nothing above it has fuel to work. We begin here.

What it looks like

Tired after a full night's sleep, foggy mornings, caffeine dependence, afternoon crash, cold hands and feet.

How we know you're ready

Stable morning energy without caffeine, deep sleep, recovery back, resting heart rate settling.

Gut. Liver. Bile.

Absorption & Detox

Your gut, liver, and the bile flow that clears hormones and toxins. You can't supplement your way out of poor absorption. Restored second.

What it looks like

Bloating that won't quit, reflux, expanding food sensitivities, skin breakouts, brain fog after meals.

How we know you're ready

Clean daily bowel movements, bloating gone, food tolerance widening, steady energy through meals.

Stress. Cortisol.

Resilience

Your nervous system, cortisol rhythm, and inflammation load. We bring the body out of survival mode so it can rebuild, not just hold.

What it looks like

Wired but tired, 3 AM wake-ups, new anxiety, racing heart at rest, unexplained irritability.

How we know you're ready

Cortisol normalizing, sleeping through the night, HRV climbing, the nervous system no longer braced.

Hormones. Thyroid.

Cycle & Signal

The full endocrine system, men and women both. Most coaching starts here. We start here only once the floor underneath holds it.

What it looks like

Irregular or missing cycles, PMS that takes you out, thyroid still low on meds, low libido, mood swings.

How we know you're ready

Cycle rhythm returning, thyroid improving across all four markers, hormone work finally landing.

Build. Hold.

Healthspan & Strength

Body composition, lean tissue, and longevity. The natural result of the system underneath finally working. Not the goal, the consequence.

What it looks like

Faster recovery, training enjoyable again, body responding to load, composition shifting without restriction.

What this phase is

The phase you stay in. We periodize training, refine nutrition, and you hold the rebuild you earned.

Why one phase at a time

We don't throw the kitchen sink at your body.

Most people arrive having had the kitchen sink thrown at them already. A supplement here, a plan there, hormones and peptides, all stacked on a floor never built to hold it. We work one phase at a time, so you see the wins, feel the shifts, and know it's moving.

"Real change is built on wins that compound, not pressure that piles up."

What to expect on the call.

A few things to know so you arrive ready.

1

The call is with our team

You'll sit with one of our Client Success Directors, trained in the same doctrine you just heard. Their job is to understand your case and see if this is the right next step.

2

No bloodwork on this call

That comes after you're a client, at intake with your coach directly. This call is for you to be heard, and for us to make sure this is the right next step.

3

Most of it is about you

Your body, your history, what hasn't worked. The questions go a little deeper than you might be used to, not to test you, but because that's usually where the rebuild starts.

4

Come honest, not polished

No script, no perfect version to perform as. Even "I don't know yet" is a real answer and a fine place to start from.

However you're arriving is welcome here. Tired, skeptical, hopeful, half in. All of it is okay.
