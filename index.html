The Sequence

Five phases.
In the order biology demands.

Each phase has a job, a way it feels when you are in it, and a clear set of signals that tell us you are ready to move forward. Nothing skipped. Nothing rushed.

M

Mitochondria

Energy. The floor.

The energy floor underneath every cell in your body. If your cells cannot make power, nothing layered on top of that has fuel to actually work. We begin here.

What this looks like

Tired after a full night's sleep. Foggy mornings. Caffeine dependence. Afternoon crash. Cold hands and feet. That feeling of never being fully online.

How we know you're ready

Morning energy stable without caffeine. Body temperature steady. Sleep deep. Recovery back. Resting heart rate dropping into a healthy floor.

A

Absorption & Detox

Gut. Liver. Bile flow.

Your gut, your liver, the bile flow that clears your hormones and your toxins. You cannot supplement your way out of poor absorption. We restore this second.

What this looks like

Bloating that does not quit. Irregular digestion. Reflux. Food sensitivities expanding. Skin breakouts. Brain fog after meals. A body that does not trust food anymore.

How we know you're ready

Clean daily bowel movements. Bloating gone. Food tolerance expanding. Skin clearing. Energy steady through meals instead of crashing after them.

R

Resilience

Stress. Cortisol. Inflammation.

Your nervous system, your cortisol rhythm, your inflammation load. Most people who arrive here have lived in survival mode for years. We let your body come out of it so it has the bandwidth to rebuild instead of just hold.

What this looks like

Wired but tired. 3 AM wake-ups. Anxiety that was not always there. Racing heart at rest. Irritability you cannot explain. A body that forgot how to relax.

How we know you're ready

Cortisol pattern normalizing. Sleeping through the night. HRV climbing. Day-to-day stress survivable again. The nervous system no longer braced.

C

Cycle & Signal

Hormones. Thyroid. Fertility.

Hormones, thyroid, fertility, the entire endocrine system, in men and women both. This is where most other coaching tries to start. We start here only once the foundation underneath is stable, because anything we layer on a broken floor comes undone.

What this looks like

Irregular or missing cycles. PMS that takes you out. Thyroid signal still low on medication. Low libido. Hormonal weight gain. Mood swings. Low drive in men. The body sending signals it cannot finish.

How we know you're ready

Cycle rhythm returning. Thyroid panel improving across all four markers. Mood stabilizing. Libido coming back. Hormone work landing because the floor finally holds it.

H

Healthspan & Strength

Build. Periodize. Hold.

Body composition. Lean tissue. Energy. Longevity. The natural consequence of the system underneath finally working. Not the goal. The result.

What this looks like

Recovery faster. Training enjoyable again. Body responding to load. Composition shifting without restriction. The body you remember, with the wisdom you have now.

What this phase is

The phase you stay in. We periodize training, refine nutrition, and you hold the rebuild you earned through everything underneath.

When we connect

What to expect
on the call.

A few things to know going in so you arrive ready.

1

The call is with our team

You will be sitting with one of our Client Success Directors. They are trained inside the same doctrine you just heard walked through. They know the framework, the process, and our standards. Their job is to understand your case more deeply and help you figure out if this is the right next step.

2

Bloodwork is not reviewed on this call

That conversation happens after you become a client, during your intake and the onboarding call with your coach directly. The call you booked is for you to be heard, and for us to make sure this is the right next step.

3

Most of the time will be about you

Your body. Your history. What brought you here. What has not worked. There will be questions that go a little deeper than what you might be used to, not to test you, but because those questions are usually where the rebuild actually starts.

4

Come honest, not polished

There is no script you need to bring. No perfect version of yourself to perform as. Even if the honest answer is "I don't know yet," that is a real answer and a fine place to start from.

However you are arriving is welcome here. Tired. Skeptical. Hopeful. Half in, half out. All of it is okay.

One last thing

We are not selling you a 30-day transformation. We are offering a structured rebuild of the system underneath everything you have been carrying.

If that is the work you have been looking for, our team is ready to meet you. And once you are in, I am here to walk with you the rest of the way.
