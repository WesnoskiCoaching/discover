What sets us apart

Straight answers.

Understand the approach.
Then decide if it feels right.

My doctor said my labs are normal. Why would this be different?

Standard reference ranges are built from population averages, and those populations include a lot of unwell people. Functional ranges ask a different question: where does a marker sit when the body is actually thriving, not just when it's short of a diagnosis.

A TSH of 4.2 is "normal." It can still explain the fatigue, the hair thinning, the cold hands. We read every marker against our in-house functional ranges, so we see the drift years before it becomes a diagnosis.

How is this different from an MD visit or a group coaching app?

A standard MD visit reads standard ranges in a 15-minute window and adjusts every six months, if that. A group coaching app hands you a static, generic plan with no one owning your case.

With us you get full functional bloodwork interpretation, one coach who owns your case, a protocol adjusted weekly or biweekly, and a team that stays in the case for 3 to 12 months and works alongside your physician. The result is a full rebuild, not symptom triage and not another template.

What actually makes the MARCH Method different?

Most programs start at hormones and thyroid, because that's what people ask for. We start at the floor. Mitochondria, then absorption, then resilience, and only then hormones. Sequence is the whole point. Hormone work stacked on a broken energy floor or a gut that can't absorb comes undone in weeks.

Can I actually get the lab testing I need?

Yes. We hold direct access to private lab testing across the US, Canada, and the UK. Your coaching team coordinates access through Niahealth in Canada, Labcorp in the US, and Youth Revisited in the UK.

Who actually reads my bloodwork and builds my plan?

Your designated coach reads your bloodwork, builds your protocol, and works with you day to day. Jimmy brings case-specific experience in autoimmune and gut health. Farren reviews every coach’s protocol before it goes out. One coach walking with you. A team behind your progress.

Are you doctors? Do you prescribe?

No. We're certified health coaches and never position ourselves as physicians. We don't diagnose, prescribe, or treat disease. We help you understand your data, build a protocol to move your markers toward optimal, and give you the language to have sharper conversations with your medical team. Any medication decision belongs to you and your physician.

What happens after I apply?

Our team reviews applications. If it looks like a fit, you’ll receive a booking link within 48 hours. Your discovery call is a conversation with the team about your goals, coaching needs, and whether we are a good fit. Bloodwork is not reviewed on that call. Lesley, our Client Success Director, helps coordinate the next steps.

How much does it cost?

Pricing depends on your case, so we walk you through it on your call.

How long is the program? Is there a minimum commitment?

The Rebuild is a 6-month minimum commitment. Rebuilding in the right order takes time.

Do you take insurance? Can I use HSA or FSA funds?

We don’t bill insurance. We can give you a receipt on request, which you can submit to your own plan or HSA or FSA provider. Whether it’s covered is up to them.

Is bloodwork included in the price?

It depends on where you live. Lab testing works differently in Canada, the United States and the United Kingdom, and your coaching team will walk you through what applies to you.

I’ve already had bloodwork done. Can you use my results?

Yes. We start with the bloodwork you already have. If markers we need are missing, we’ll tell you which ones and help you get them.

How soon will I notice a difference?

It varies from person to person, so we don’t promise a timeline. We review your biofeedback at every check-in so you can see what’s shifting.

Can I do this if I’m on medication, HRT or TRT?

Yes. We coach alongside your medical care. We never start, stop or change a medication. That stays between you and your physician.

Can you help with Hashimoto’s or thyroid issues?

Yes. We coach people with Hashimoto’s and thyroid concerns, alongside their medical care. We don’t diagnose or treat. We read your bloodwork against functional ranges and build your plan around it.

Can you help with perimenopause?

Yes. We coach women through perimenopause, alongside their medical care. We don’t diagnose or treat.

How often do I talk to my coach?

You have weekly or bi-weekly check-ins, direct in-app access to your coach between calls, and live office hours with the team twice a month.

Who will my coach be?

Lesley, our Client Success Director, matches you with a designated coach based on your location and coaching needs. Farren reviews every protocol before it goes out.

Do you work with men?

Yes. We work with women and men.

Do I have to live in Canada, the US or the UK?

We arrange lab testing in Canada, the United States and the United Kingdom. If you live somewhere else, you’d need to get your own bloodwork done locally.

Will I have to take a lot of supplements?

Supplements are chosen for your case, not handed out as a standard stack. They’re bought separately from coaching.

Do I have to follow a strict diet or training plan?

No template plans. Your plan is built around your bloodwork, routines and goals.

What to expect
on the call.

A few things to know going in so you arrive ready.

Before you join

  • Check the date, time and time zone in your confirmation email.
  • Keep your meeting link handy and join from somewhere quiet.
  • Bring your questions and think about what you most want help with.
  • Need to change your booking? Reply to your confirmation email.
1

The call is with our team

You will meet with the team member assigned to your booking. Together, you will talk through what brought you here, what you want help with, and whether our coaching is the right next step.

2

Bloodwork is not reviewed on this call

That conversation happens after you become a client, during your intake and the onboarding call with your coach directly. The call you booked is for you to be heard, and for us to make sure this is the right next step.

3

Most of the time will be about you

Your body. Your history. What brought you here. What has not worked. There will be questions that go a little deeper than what you might be used to, not to test you, but because those questions are usually where the rebuild actually starts.

4

Come honest, not polished

There is no script you need to bring. No perfect version of yourself to perform as. Even if the honest answer is "I don't know yet," that is a real answer and a fine place to start from.

However you are arriving is welcome here. Tired. Skeptical. Hopeful. Half in, half out. All of it is okay.
